Wi-Fi over USB

Set up Wi-Fi on your ESP-Hub board through the USB cable. Your computer stays on its own network.

This browser cannot talk to USB devices

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari, Firefox and phones do not support Web Serial.

1. Connect the board

  1. Plug the board in with a USB data cable.
  2. Close Thonny, serial monitors and other tabs that use the board.
  3. Click Connect and pick the board (CP210x, CH340 or USB serial).

2. Enter your Wi-Fi

The board only supports 2.4 GHz networks. Leave the password empty for open Wi-Fi.
